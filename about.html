The shop

Martha arranges in the morning.

Martha's Flowers is imaginary. The photos are of real bouquets. There is no account, no server, and no charge. The cart, the saved list, and the last search live in this browser.

Delivery

Orders before noon go out the same day, in town only. After noon they go the next morning. Over $80, the wrap is included. Under that, it is paper and a rubber band.

What stays in the browser

martha.cart and martha.wishlist are in localStorage, so they survive a refresh. martha.session remembers the last few bouquets you opened and the last search. The checkout draft and the receipt are in sessionStorage, so they go away when the tab closes. The card number is not saved.
